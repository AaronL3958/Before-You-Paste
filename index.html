05 / THE ETHICS BEHIND THE PAUSE

There’s a person
behind the information.

Convenience for one person can create risk for someone else. Responsible AI use asks whose interests are being protected—and whose are being overlooked.

◎

Privacy & autonomy

People have an interest in how information about them is used. Having access to a message or document does not automatically mean you should share it with another service.

↔

Benefits & possible harm

Useful context can improve an answer. Balance that benefit against possible embarrassment, exposure, loss of trust, or harm to people who never chose to use the tool.

⌘

Professional responsibility

The ACM Code of Ethics calls on computing professionals to respect privacy and protect confidential information. These duties matter before a problem occurs.

Read the ACM code ↗
Who has something at stake?

You, the person described in your prompt, classmates and research participants, your school or employer, and the people designing and operating AI systems. People whose information is shared without their knowledge may have little control over what happens next.

A FEW IMPORTANT QUESTIONS

Does everything I type become public?

No universal rule applies to every AI service. Data handling varies by provider, product, account, settings, and agreement. Don’t assume a prompt is public—or confidential. Check the relevant policy and your organization’s approved-use guidance.

Is removing a name enough?

Not always. A combination of details—such as a unique role, precise date, location, or personal event—may still identify a person. Remove unnecessary context and consider whether sharing the material is permitted at all.

What if I already shared something sensitive?

Stop sharing it, review the service’s available deletion and privacy controls, and follow your school or employer’s reporting process when applicable. If a password or API key was exposed, revoke or change it through the appropriate account or administrator. Deleting a visible chat is not a universal guarantee that all copies are removed.

Does this website inspect my prompts?

No. This is an educational guide, not a privacy scanner. There is no prompt upload box and no AI integration. Checklist choices and practice answers stay in this browser session and reset when you reload. The website itself adds no analytics or advertising trackers. The hosting service may process ordinary access and security logs.
