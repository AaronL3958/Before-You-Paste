# Before You Paste

**A little pause. A lot more privacy.**

Before You Paste is an interactive guide that helps students make more thoughtful decisions about the information they share with AI tools. It shows how to get useful help while leaving out unnecessary personal or confidential details.

**[Visit the website](https://aaronl3958.github.io/Before-You-Paste/)**

No account, download, or installation is needed to use the live website.

## Why this project exists

Asking AI for help can involve more than sharing a question. A résumé, screenshot, private conversation, or research document may contain information about people who never agreed to have it shared with another service.

This guide encourages a simple habit: pause before sending, consider whose information is included, and decide what is actually necessary for the task.

## What you can do

- **Recognize sensitive details.** Learn what to look for in text, files, and screenshots, including identifying information, private conversations, and confidential work.
- **Explore better prompts.** Compare fictional before-and-after examples involving group projects, résumés, debugging code, and research notes.
- **Use the checklist.** Review five questions about necessity, identification, permission, tool settings, and attachments. Print it to keep nearby.
- **Practice making decisions.** Work through three short situations and read the explanation behind each choice.
- **Understand the ethics.** Explore privacy, consent, possible harm, and professional responsibility, then follow links to primary sources.

## Start here

1. Open the website and read **“More personal than you might think.”**
2. Choose a situation under **“A better way to ask.”** Compare the original prompt with the revised version and read why it changed.
3. Review the **Before-You-Paste Checklist** before your next AI request. Checking a box means you considered the question; it does not certify that sharing is safe.
4. Try **“What would you leave out?”** to practice applying the ideas.
5. Visit **Resources** for further guidance.

## A simple example

**Before**

> Help me write an email about my classmate Jordan Smith, student ID 12345678, who missed our meeting because of their medical treatment.

**After**

> Help me write a respectful email about a group member who missed a meeting. Focus on rescheduling and dividing the remaining work.

The revised prompt keeps the task while removing details that are not needed to complete it. All people and identifying details in the website’s examples are fictional.

## Privacy and scope

This website is an educational guide, not an AI chatbot or an automated privacy scanner. It does not ask you to upload documents or paste real prompts.

- Checklist selections and practice answers stay in the browser session and reset when the page reloads.
- The website does not submit those answers or include a feedback collection form.
- The application adds no analytics or advertising trackers. GitHub Pages may process ordinary hosting and security logs.
- Copying an example places its fictional text on your clipboard; it does not send it to an AI service.

Removing a name does not guarantee anonymity. Other details can still identify someone. AI services also differ in how they handle information, so check the specific product’s policies and settings, along with any school, research, or workplace requirements. The guide cannot guarantee confidentiality or authorize sharing restricted material.
